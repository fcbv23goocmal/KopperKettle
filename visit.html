Good to know

Before you come in.

Do you take reservations?

No — it's a diner. Come in and grab a booth or a counter stool. At the 7–9am rush there can be a short wait, but tables turn quickly.

What time should I come for breakfast?

Doors open at 5:00 am on weekdays and Saturday. The first hour is the quietest; by 7:30 the room is full and the biscuits are flying.

Is there parking?

Yes — a lot in front of the building, plus overflow around the side. It's an industrial-park stretch of Nations Ford Rd, so there's room.

Can I get takeout or call ahead?

Yes. Call (704) 588-1164 for takeout, biscuits by the dozen, or a catering tray. Give us a little notice for big orders.

Are you open on Sunday?

No. Sunday is the one day the grill stays off. We're back Monday at 5:00 am.

What's livermush?

A Western North Carolina pork and cornmeal loaf, sliced and griddled. It's the local breakfast you should try at least once — order it with eggs and grits.
